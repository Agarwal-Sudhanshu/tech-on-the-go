Tech On The Go

Privacy policy

Tech On The Go is a daily technology briefing published as a video and a podcast.

The Tech On The Go Uploader is a private tool used only by the channel owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. No one else can sign in to it.

The tool does not collect, store or share personal data from viewers, listeners or any other person. The only credential it holds is the owner's own authorisation to upload to their channel, which is stored privately and can be revoked at any time in the owner's Google account settings.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses as described in GitHub's own privacy statement. The site itself sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Contact: open an issue on the project page.
